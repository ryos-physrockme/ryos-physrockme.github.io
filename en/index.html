Field theory &
particle physics.

I study integrability and machine learning in field theory, nonlinear field dynamics including oscillons, and particle physics with extra dimensions and lepton flavor violation.

Research interestsFIELD THEORY · PARTICLE PHYSICS

01

Integrability & machine learning

I combine machine learning with analytic verification to search for Lax connections and integrable structures in field theories.

02

Nonlinear field dynamics

I study long-lived, localized oscillatory solutions known as oscillons, including their dynamics in anti-de Sitter (AdS) spacetime.

03

Particle theory & phenomenology

My interests include model building through coset space dimensional reduction and lepton flavor violation in true muonium decays.
